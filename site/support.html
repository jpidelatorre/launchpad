Support & Quick Guide

Need a hand getting the most out of Launchpad? Here is everything you need to know.

Getting Started

  • Choose Your Folder: The first time you open a New Tab, pick which bookmark folder you want to display. You can change this anytime by clicking the gear icon (,) in the bottom toolbar.
  • Select Multiple Items: Drag a box on empty space around several items—or hold Shift while clicking—to open the floating action bar above the bottom toolbar. From there you can open them all, group them into a new folder, or delete them.
  • Customize Your View: Open Settings to switch between Comfortable, Compact, List, or App Icon views, adjust icon sizes, and pick your theme and accent color.
